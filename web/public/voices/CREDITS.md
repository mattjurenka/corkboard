# Voices

The recordings each voice is cloned from (bridge/speech.ts), and the clips.

- `af_anime.wav`, `af_anime.bin`, `clips/`: cicifyre, "Free Voice Clips Pack (Bright Female)", itch.io.
- `am_puck.wav`: Sean Lenhart's lines from "Super Dialogue Audio Pack" by Dillon Becker,
  https://dillonbecker.itch.io/sdap, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); joined and trimmed.
- `am_fenrir.wav`: Ernst Pattynama reading Sir Philip Sidney's "My True Love", LibriVox,
  https://librivox.org/my-true-love-by-sir-philip-sidney, public domain; trimmed.
- `bm_george.wav`: Adrian Praetzellis reading John Buchan's "The Thirty-Nine Steps", chapter 1, LibriVox,
  https://librivox.org/the-thirty-nine-steps-by-john-buchan/, public domain; trimmed.
- `af_bella.wav`: kaylawhy reading Shelley's "Drama" in "Love Poems by Famous Authors", section 24, LibriVox,
  https://librivox.org/love-poems-by-famous-authors-by-various/, public domain; trimmed.
- `af_nicole.wav`: Elizabeth Klett reading Charlotte Brontë's "Villette", chapter 2, LibriVox,
  https://librivox.org/villette-by-charlotte-bronte/, public domain; trimmed.
- `bm_fable.wav`: Tadhg Hynes reading Charles Dickens's "Oliver Twist", chapter 2, LibriVox,
  https://archive.org/details/oliver_twist_th_librivox, public domain; trimmed.
- `af_heart.wav`: Kara Shallenberg reading Richmal Crompton's "More William", chapter 2, "Rice-Mould", LibriVox,
  https://archive.org/details/more_william_ks_librivox, public domain; trimmed.
- `bf_lily.wav`: Lizzie Driver reading Jonathan Swift's "Gulliver's Travels", part 1, chapter 1, LibriVox,
  https://archive.org/details/gulliver_ld_librivox, public domain; trimmed, and raised 1.2 times in pitch at its own pace.
- `am_eric.wav`: Phil Chenevert reading H. P. Lovecraft's "The Colour Out of Space", part 1, LibriVox,
  https://archive.org/details/thecolouroutofspace_2206_librivox, public domain; trimmed.
